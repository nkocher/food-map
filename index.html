Cooking Issues Picks

    This page doesn't store any map data itself. You load it from Google onto this device, and you do it again whenever you want the latest picks.

    1. While signed in to Google, open the live KML download. On iPhone, save it to Files. (Getting a 403 or an empty file? Your Patreon Google account isn't your default one, so try 2nd account or 3rd.)
    2. Tap Load KML below and pick that file.
    3. Optional: Share → Add to Home Screen.
